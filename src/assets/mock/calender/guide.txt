================================================================================
FOLDER: src/assets/mock/calendar/
PURPOSE: Tippani Monthly Gregorian + Hindu Tithi Calendar & Events
================================================================================

1. FOLDER STRUCTURE (Important: folder name is 'calendar', not 'calender')
------------------------------------------------------------------------
src/assets/mock/calendar/
  └── <year>/                (e.g., 2026, 2027)
        ├── january.json
        ├── february.json
        └── ...              (lowercase English month names)

3. STEP-BY-STEP FOR NEW TENANT
------------------------------
Step 1: Create a year folder (e.g., 2026/) under src/assets/mock/calendar/.
Step 2: Add lowercase <month>.json files (e.g., november.json, december.json).
Step 3: Populate "days" (1..31) with "hinduDay", "hinduTithiShort", and optional
        "events" (with optional targetBookId/targetChapterId deep links).
